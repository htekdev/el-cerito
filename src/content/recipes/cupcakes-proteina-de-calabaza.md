instructionsEn:
  - "Preheat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and line 6 wells of a cupcake pan with liners. Spray them with cooking spray so nothing sticks."
  - "In a bowl, whisk the pumpkin purée, granulated monk fruit, egg whites, vanilla, cinnamon, and pumpkin spice until smooth."
  - "Add the oat flour, 1 scoop of birthday cake protein, and baking soda. Mix just until no lumps remain."
  - "Divide the batter evenly between the 6 liners, filling them almost to the top."
  - "In another bowl, whip the Greek yogurt, low-fat cream cheese, 1/2 scoop of protein, and powdered monk fruit into a smooth frosting."
  - "Transfer the frosting to a piping bag (or a zip bag with the tip cut off) and pipe it into the center of each cupcake. Top with chocolate chips or pumpkin seeds if you like."
  - "Bake for 22–23 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean."
  - "Let them cool a few minutes before eating — enjoy fall without the guilt!"

Un antojito de otoño que sí cuadra con los macros: cupcakes de calabaza con betún
cremoso de yogur griego y queso crema, inyectado justo en el centro. Sin harina de
trigo, endulzados con monkfruit y con un scoop de proteína en polvo sabor birthday
cake — unas 90 kcal y 11 g de proteína cada uno. Ideales para el antojo de
*pumpkin spice* sin salirse del plan.
